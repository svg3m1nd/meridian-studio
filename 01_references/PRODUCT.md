# Product Reference

## Name

Meridian Studio

## Product promise

Show how a business is understood, retrieved, cited, compared, and trusted across modern search systems—with every conclusion traceable to evidence.

## Primary users

- Agencies managing many client accounts
- In-house search, content, brand, and communications teams
- Analysts researching retrieval behavior
- White-label partners delivering branded intelligence portals
- Executives consuming evidence-backed summaries and action plans

## Core jobs

1. Capture canonical business truth.
2. Observe external sources and retrieval systems repeatedly.
3. Resolve entities, claims, queries, sources, and passages into connected graphs.
4. Record independent signals without forcing premature aggregation.
5. Evaluate evidence states across configurable layers.
6. Compare client and competitor representations.
7. Explain gaps with source-level provenance.
8. Turn findings into prioritized actions.
9. Export and share branded client-facing views.

## Prototype-derived views

- Pattern histogram
- Layer detail
- EAV baseline
- Mirror comparison
- Evidence log
- Action plan
- Trust and spam gate

These are projections over canonical data. They are not separate stores.

## Multi-tenant boundary

An organization is the commercial tenant. An organization owns users, brand settings, domains, signal definitions, and client workspaces. Every client workspace owns its graphs, evidence, sources, runs, findings, competitors, and actions.

## White-label boundary

Product name, logo, favicon, colors, support identity, email templates, report covers, and verified custom domains are tenant configuration. White-labeling must not fork application code or database schemas.

## Non-goals for the first release

- Autonomous publishing to client sites
- A universal or proprietary “SEO score”
- Fully automated strategic recommendations without review
- Billing complexity beyond basic subscription entitlement
- Arbitrary third-party plugin execution inside the core process
